Application logo PNGs preserve the original marks from the Devicon project:
https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons

Source paths: react/react-original.svg, nextjs/nextjs-original.svg,
typescript/typescript-original.svg, tailwindcss/tailwindcss-original.svg,
supabase/supabase-original.svg, threejs/threejs-original.svg,
github/github-original.svg, vercel/vercel-original.svg, figma/figma-original.svg,
python/python-original.svg, nodejs/nodejs-original.svg, canva/canva-original.svg,
git/git-original.svg.

Motion uses the official website favicon: https://motion.dev/favicon.ico

The assets are converted to 128 px PNG for this website. No SVG is used to
render application logos. The neutral education and generic tool placeholders
are locally authored PNG assets and do not represent any institution or brand.
